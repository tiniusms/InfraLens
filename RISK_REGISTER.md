# InfraLens risk register

This register records measured failures and remaining limitations of the delivered prototype. Severity is a qualitative limit on permissible analyst use. The register does not assign numerical probabilities, weighted scores, site rankings, or investment recommendations.

R1 and R2 are measured results of the one-shot EIA holdout. R3 through R9 are methodological or operational limitations. The nine rows are not nine equally measured failures. Existing disclosures reduce misuse. They leave the underlying limitations in place.

`PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` remains the planned-scope statement. This file does not replace it and does not change `DECISIONS.md`.
